About this compilation

An article-by-article view of Law No. 27 of 2026 on Employment Protection (UUPK), enacted and promulgated on 28 October 2026 (State Gazette 2026 No. 179), set against Law No. 13 of 2003 on Manpower as amended by Law No. 6 of 2023 (UUK) and the implementing and related regulations that bear on each Pasal.

How to read a Pasal

  • UUPK: the new text, with its Elucidation (Penjelasan) when switched on.
  • Redline: UUPK compared word by word with the matching UUK provision. Blue is new wording, red strikethrough is UUK wording that was removed or replaced. Capitalisation differences are ignored.
  • UUK: the corresponding provision of the previous law.
  • Regulations: implementing and related regulations in force that address the same subject. Mapping is editorial and not exhaustive.
  • The coloured dot next to a Pasal in the contents shows whether it is new (no UUK counterpart), changed, or unchanged apart from capitalisation.

Views

Show one to three Pasal at once. Columns places Pasal side by side, Rows stacks Pasal and lines the panels up across (best for comparing one Pasal), and Tabs shows one panel at a time. Switch panels on and off, change the text size, and use the arrow keys (Left, Right) to move between Pasal and / to search. Links such as #p81 open a specific Pasal.

Interactive features

  • Cross-references: every "Pasal N" mentioned in the UUPK text is a dotted link. Click it to preview that Pasal, jump to it, or open it beside the current one.
  • Redline modes: inside the Redline panel switch between Markup, Clean (UUPK only) and Original (UUK only). Click a panel title to collapse it.
  • Overview (grid icon): a heat map of all Pasal by degree of change, with totals per BAB. Click any cell to open it.
  • Regulations show where else the same provision is cited. The star on a Pasal bookmarks it in this browser.
  • Search covers UUPK, UUK and the regulation texts. Try uuk 57 to find the Pasal that replaces Pasal 57 of the UUK.
  • Shortcuts: / search, ← → or [ ] previous or next, 1 2 3 number of Pasal shown, o overview, s star, d theme, ? this window.

Please note

  • This is an unofficial working compilation and not legal advice. Check the official text before relying on it.
  • The UUPK text was transcribed from the promulgated copy using OCR and checked with automated comparisons only. Residual transcription errors are possible.
  • Some regulation texts are taken from consolidated versions (marked "consolidated") and may be shortened where very long.
  • Implementing regulations under the UUPK are still to be issued (within one year under Pasal 312). Existing regulations continue to apply to the extent they do not conflict with the UUPK (Pasal 310).
